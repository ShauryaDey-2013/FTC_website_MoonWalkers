Engineering Excellence Driven by Passion & Innovation

We are MoonWalkers #38056, an ambitious FIRST Tech Challenge team. Transitioning from years of competitive FIRST LEGO League (FLL) championships, our team brings deep experience in custom mechanical design, autonomous path planning, and community outreach.

What started as a small group of elementary students tinkering with LEGO bricks 5 years ago has evolved into a high-powered FIRST Tech Challenge engineering team. Through hundreds of late-night coding sessions, chassis redesigns, and regional competitions, our bond as lifelong friends has remained our greatest strength.

As new members joined our roster, they brought fresh perspectives in 3D CAD modeling, autonomous pathfinding, and community outreach. Today, Team #38056 is dedicated to pushing the boundaries of competitive FTC robotics while mentoring the next generation of STEM enthusiasts.

Our mission extends beyond building competitive robots. We strive to cultivate an inclusive environment where students master real-world STEM disciplines—from goBILDA chassis assembly and REV Control Hub Java programming to CAD design and sensor telemetry calibration.
